# OpenGLESScope Database 0.2.2

OpenGLESScope Database 0.2.2 completes Android security-patch end-to-end parity with OpenGLESScope 0.2.2 and VulkanScope. Current 0.2.2 producers must submit canonical `device.securityPatch` and matching TXT evidence; older compatible producers remain readable and accepted according to their historical contract.
